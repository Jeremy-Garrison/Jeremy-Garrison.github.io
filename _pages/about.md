I am a PhD candidate and Graduate Teaching Instructor in the Department of Economics at [Colorado State University](https://economics.colostate.edu/). I hold a master's degree in Economics from CSU and bachelor's degrees in Environmental Policy and Mathematics from [the University of Tulsa](https://utulsa.edu/). 

My research focuses on evolutionary game theory, social economics, and cultural economics. I develop theoretical models and employ data to explore the relationship between economic ecology and the evolution and behavioral expression of preferences. My dissertation investigates the sociocultural and behavioral consequences of markets and capitalism through theoretical models using cultural evolution, game theory, and networks as well as empirical evidence from folklore, ethnography, and experimental economics. In the future, I aim to extend these methods to questions of stratification economics, ecological economics, and cliodynamics. More information can be found on my [research](research) page.
